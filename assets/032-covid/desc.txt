Amsterdam
2000 / 2020 – by [Your Name]

This painting is based on a photograph taken in Amsterdam in 2000. Multiple shots were taken to capture people as they appeared in different windows; each figure in the painting represents a separate moment in time. Reflections in the glass, including the artist’s own, add another layer to the work. Inspired by the distinct architecture and the idea of living in small, compartmentalised spaces, the final painting was completed in 2020.
