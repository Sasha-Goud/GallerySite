Amsterdam - 2000-2020

This work began with a photograph of a building in Amsterdam in 2000. Multiple shots captured figures appearing in different windows, each representing a moment in time. Reflections in the glass — including the artist’s own — add another layer of time and space. The final painting was completed in 2020.
