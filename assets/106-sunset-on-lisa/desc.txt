Mimic - 1985

Mimic explores repeating shapes around us, appearing in different contexts yet sharing the same visual logic. The result is a minimal photograph that plays with form and perception. The curves of a reclining body are set against a stylised sunset behind mountain peaks, creating a quiet harmony between human contour and landscape, so subtle that, at first glance, the body, though in the foreground, is almost invisible.
