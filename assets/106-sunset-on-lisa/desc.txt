Mimic
1985 – by [Your Name]

This image was created in 1985 as part of a photographic study exploring how the human form can echo the shapes of nature. Here, the curve of a reclining body is set against a stylized landscape, blending figure and environment into a single composition. The scene was entirely constructed in-studio using cut paper, a real person, and studio blinds. The result is a minimal and graphic image that plays with form and perception.
