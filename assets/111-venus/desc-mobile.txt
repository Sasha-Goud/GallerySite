Venus - 1978 - 2005

From my Still in Motion series, this work uses long exposure and camera movement — “painting with the camera.” Shot in 1978 and reworked digitally in 2005, it was reduced to a near-abstract image that still conveys movement and presence, with a neutral palette suited to almost any setting.
