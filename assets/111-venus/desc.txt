Venus, 1978

This piece is part of my 'Still in Motion' photography series, where I combined long exposure with deliberate camera movement—what I often think of as “painting with the camera.” The original photograph was taken in 1978 as part of an exploration into motion and body form through analog means.

Years later, with the introduction of digital editing, I returned to the image, not to enhance it, but to reduce it. I stripped away much of the colour and detail, minimising the image to just what was needed for recognition. What remains is a light, nearly abstract impression that still conveys movement and presence, while allowing the viewer’s imagination to fill in the rest.
