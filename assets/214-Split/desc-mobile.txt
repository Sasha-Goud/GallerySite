Bipolarity - 2000

This piece begun in early 2000 and completed in December to mark the new millennium, it was created in short sessions over ten months, woven into the rhythms of everyday life. It reflects the world’s bipolar nature, opposing forces held in balance, while ultimately aiming for bold, colourful graphics that bring life to a wall.
