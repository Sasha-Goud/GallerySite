Bipolarity, 2000

This piece began in early 2000 and was completed later that same year—intentionally finished within the calendar year to mark the new millennium. It was worked on in short, scattered sessions over about ten months, fitting into the natural interruptions of everyday life.

The painting reflects the bipolar nature of the world around us: north-south, east-west, rich-poor, black-white, have-have-not. For me, both sides are equally valuable, different but balanced. There’s no hierarchy. At its heart, though, the aim was simple: to create bold, colourful graphics that bring life to a wall.
