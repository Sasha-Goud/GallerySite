
Positano
1999 – 2012

This piece began as a 35mm digital photograph taken in Positano, Italy, in 1999. The image was later posterised in 2012 to bring out the sharp lines and bold shapes of the sunlit architecture and plant forms. The result is a graphic composition that blends realism with abstraction while keeping the spirit of the original scene.
