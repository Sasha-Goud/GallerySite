You 2003


Although most of my work is driven by graphic composition, every now and then something compels me to manifest an idea more directly. This piece represents how I imagine life: intersecting dimensions, each its own universe. Where they all overlap is the life of the individual. That point of convergence is the viewer, in that moment. Even while viewing the painting, all dimensions are active, creating a one-time connection between the image and the person standing in front of it.

This is a large acrylic on canvas. Due to the sculptural texture at its center, it cannot be faithfully reproduced and is only available as an original.
