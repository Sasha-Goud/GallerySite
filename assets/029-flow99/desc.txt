Flow 99 - 1991

This work is part of a series of accidental aesthetics. The idea behind the work is simple: physical reactions create forms, and those forms often repeat themselves. Whether it’s melted cheese, the surface of a planet, or the structure of a cell, these visual patterns connect the smallest moments to the largest scales of the universe.
