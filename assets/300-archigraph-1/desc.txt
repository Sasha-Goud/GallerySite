SE1

Based on a photograph I took in 2004, this piece was completed as a posterised image in 2023. The original scene, shot along London’s South Bank, already had a striking composition of lines, reflections, and colour blocks. Posterising it enhanced the graphic quality I saw in the moment.

This piece works especially well as a Giclée Hahnemühle Photo Rag print. It’s a more expensive paper, but worth it — the image truly comes to life on its surface. The super-matte finish enhances muted blacks, preserves fine detail, and offers even colour reproduction. Its subtle texture and smooth, cotton feel create beautifully soft colour gradients.

Available as a print on canvas in three sizes, framed or unframed.
