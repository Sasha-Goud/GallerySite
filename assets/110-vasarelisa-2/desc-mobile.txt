Binomial - 1984

As a teenager, I explored geometric art until discovering Victor Vasarely and M. C. Escher. Realising the form had already reached its limits, I redirected that geometry into photography. This piece, an Escher projection across the human body, was finally realised in 1984 after years of experimentation.
