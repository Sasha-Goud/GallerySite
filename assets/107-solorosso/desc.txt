Sole
2007–2018 – by [Your Name]

Part of the Unique Moments series, this image was taken in a nearly empty restaurant in Paris in 2007, late in the afternoon. The sunlight came in at just the right angle, casting a strong diagonal of light across the scene. One single rose stood in its path, while the others remained in shadow. The neutral colours of the background, the stillness of the space, and the natural composition all came together to create a graphic image that was entirely unplanned. The photograph was posterised in 2018 to highlight these contrasts even further.
