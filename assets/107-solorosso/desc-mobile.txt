Sole - 2007–2018

From my Unique Moments series, this photograph captures a late afternoon in a near-empty Parisian restaurant in 2007. A diagonal shaft of sunlight lights a single rose, leaving the others in shadow, creating a graphic image that feels almost staged. It was posterised in 2018 to heighten the contrast for final production.
