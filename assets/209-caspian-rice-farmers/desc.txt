Rice, 2001–2014

Based on an original photograph taken on the shores of the Caspian Sea, this image depicts local women working on a rice farm. The composition remains faithful to the original moment, but while the photograph might be seen as interesting or documentary, this version transforms it into a bold, decorative graphic with strong visual impact.

Available as a print on canvas in three sizes.
