Picasso, 2002

If I had to name the artists who most impressed me as a young boy, three come to mind; Dalí, Picasso and Gaudí This is the Dali portrait. There’s also a matching one of Picasso.

It took me years to figure out how to pay tribute to them visually. I didn’t want to simply paint a portrait, I was waiting for the right idea. This concept finally came together in 2002. As for Gaudí, I still haven’t found the right approach, and I’m not sure if I ever will.

These are large acrylic paintings on canvas and are available as originals, as well as replica prints on canvas
