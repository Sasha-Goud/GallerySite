Dali - 2002

Three artists impressed me most as a young boy: Dalí, Picasso, and Gaudí. This is the Dali portrait, with a matching one of Picasso.

It took me years to find a way to pay tribute visually. It finally came together after 30 years, in 2002. As for Gaudí, I still haven’t found the right approach, and I’m not sure I ever will.
