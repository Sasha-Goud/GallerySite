Accidental Aesthetics I
1991– by [Your Name]

This work is part of a series of accidental aesthetics, where natural or unexpected patterns serve as the starting point for abstract compositions. In this piece, the textures were inspired by the way cheese expands under foil during cooking. As the foil is peeled away, it reveals intricate surface patterns—formed through heat and pressure—that echo the shapes we see across nature.

The idea behind the work is simple: physical reactions create forms, and those forms often repeat themselves. Whether it’s melted cheese, the surface of a planet, or the structure of a cell, these visual patterns connect the smallest moments to the largest scales of the universe.
