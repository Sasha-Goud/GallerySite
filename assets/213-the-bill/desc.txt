Duch, 1997

Based on a photograph I took in Covent Garden in 1997, this image captures a group of people at the moment they’re Splitting the bill
 after a meal. None of them knew they were forming such a striking composition, one I found immediately graphic yet full of life.

It’s one of those candid moments where real life arranges itself into art without trying.
