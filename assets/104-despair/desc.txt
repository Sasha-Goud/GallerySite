This photograph was taken in 1976 and is part of two ongoing bodies of work: the Motion series and the Monochromic Body Form series. Though captured in color, the image appears nearly monochrome, with only a faint hint of tint. This effect is a result of long exposure combined with intentional camera movement, creating a blurred gesture that captures emotion through form rather than detail.
