South Bank 1998–2012

Part of a series that grew out of my fascination with bicycles, their exposed structure has an engineering elegance that’s both functional and beautiful.

This image was photographed in 1998 on the South Bank of the River Thames, looking across to the city lights. In 2012, I revisited it and transformed it digitally into the stylised piece you see here.
