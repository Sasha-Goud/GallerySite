Moment
1999 – by [Your Name]

This photograph was taken from a distance using a telephoto lens and is part of the Unique Moments series. It captures two strangers, completely unaware of each other, who have inadvertently become part of the same composition. The result is an unplanned moment of visual balance—an accidental graphic formed by timing, movement, and perspective. The grainy texture and color noise were intentional, created by using the wrong film speed, handheld shooting, and overextended zoom—techniques used here to heighten the raw, observational nature of the piece.
