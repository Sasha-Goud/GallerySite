Moment - 1999

Part of my Unique Moments series, this photograph captures two strangers, unaware of each other, drawn into the same frame to create an unplanned balance of timing, movement, and perspective. There is no digital manipulation, the effects come from handheld shooting, pushing the zoom beyond comfort, and using the wrong film speed to heighten the raw, observational feel of the image.
