
Resonance

This image was captured in 1976 as part of a photographic experiment titled Still in Motion. It was created using long exposure and a technique similar to painting with the camera—by moving the camera rather than the subject. No digital editing was used.

Resonance is one of two works from the original series featured in this collection, with a third appearing later.
