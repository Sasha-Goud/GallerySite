
Resonance

This piece belongs to my 'Still in Motion' series, a body of work I was deeply engaged in during the 1970s as I explored ways of capturing movement within a still image. It was created using long exposure and a technique similar to painting with the camera, by moving the camera rather than the subject. No digital editing was used.

Resonance is one of two works from the original series featured in this collection, with a third appearing later.
