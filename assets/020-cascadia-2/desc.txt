La Chiesa Madre 1999


Based on a photograph I took in Positano in 1999, this piece began with a compositional idea, using a telephoto lens to flatten the perspective, emphasising the cascading geometry typical of the town’s architecture. I painted the image in grayscale acrylic on board to heighten its accented sunset reflections.

The original has been sold, but replica prints on canvas are available in three sizes, framed or unframed.
