Persepolis - 2001

Photographed just outside the ruins of Persepolis in southern Iran, this image contrasts thousands of years of history with a modern car park behind the camera. While the ruins have been captured countless times, this perspective is unique. Shot entirely on film with no digital manipulation, the effect comes purely from exposure, depth of field, and film speed. Particularly striking as a Giclée Hahnemühle German Etching print, its velvety surface holds the ink and light beautifully.
