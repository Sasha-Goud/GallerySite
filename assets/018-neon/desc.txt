CONTOUR (1978)

Photographic print from original analog experiment shot on 35 mm film.

Created in 1978 as part of a series of light experiments using a projector and two torches, sculpting the edge of the body with minimal illumination. In the image, emptiness shapes what is seen: a tension between light and void.

MEDIA:
- Type: C-Type Fuji Flex (Super Gloss)
- Weight: 255 GSM
- Tone: Warm White
- Finish: Ultra-high gloss with deep blacks and rich contrast

Fuji Flex lends the image a vivid, cinematic quality, with intense highlights, deep blacks and a reflective surface that accentuates every curve of light.

DISPLAY TIP:
For best results, display on evenly lit walls and avoid direct spotlights to minimise glare.
