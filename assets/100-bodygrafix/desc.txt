Form and Contrast
1975 / 1998 – by [Your Name]

This painting is based on a photograph taken in 1975 and completed as a mixed media piece in 1998. It is part of a series that explores the human body through simple, graphic compositions. These works often use just two tones to focus on shape rather than detail. Negative space, empty areas suggesting as much as the visible forms. What’s left out is completed by the viewer, yet still communicates emotion and presence.
