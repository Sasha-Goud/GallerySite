♀, 2025


This is one of two paintings, this version represents male, and the other, female. The pair takes a tongue-in-cheek look at behaviours associated with gender, but the real aim here is colour, rhythm, and movement.

Unlike much of my work, which evolves slowly over time, these were both conceived and completed within a week—uncharacteristic for me, but refreshing in its spontaneity.

Though they were designed as a pair, each painting also works entirely on its own.
