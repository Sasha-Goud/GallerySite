Binomial - 1984

As a teenager in the 1960s, I was fascinated by geometric art and made it in various media. Later, when I discovered Victor Vasarely and M. C. Escher, I realised what I was exploring had already been done—brilliantly and completely—so I stopped pursuing it as a visual art form.

Instead, I carried those ideas into photography. This image comes from that shift: an Escher projection cast onto the human body. It plays with light, void, and form, prompting the viewer to complete what’s missing. After years of experiments in the 1970s, I finally captured it as I’d imagined it in 1984.
