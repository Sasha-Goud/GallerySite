Moment - 1999

Taken with a telephoto lens, this piece is part of my Unique Moments series. It captures two strangers, unaware of each other, who have inadvertently fallen into the same frame, creating an unplanned moment of visual balance: an accidental graphic shaped by timing, movement, and perspective.

The grain and colour noise are intentional, created by the wrong film speed, handheld shooting, and pushing the zoom beyond comfort, choices made to heighten the raw, observational feel of the image.
