Persepolis - 2001

This photograph was taken just outside the ruins of Persepolis in southern Iran. In front of me: two-and-a-half thousand years of history—and hundreds of cameras pointing that way. Behind me: a newly built car park. The ruins have been photographed millions of times; this view, I think, is unique.

Shot on film, it’s entirely analogue, with no digital manipulation. Everything you see was created in-camera through exposure, depth of field, and film speed.

Available on canvas, but especially striking as a Giclée Hahnemühle German Etching print. The paper’s rich velvety texture holds more ink and catches the light beautifully.
