Le Marais - 1978–2023

Photographed in Le Marais in 1978, this piece was later printed and worked over in acrylic on board. Warm façades and cool shadows create the structure, while the lamp and the small car hold the eye in place. The result is a hybrid image, moody, but upbeat, where texture and colour turn a sleepy street into a lively image.
