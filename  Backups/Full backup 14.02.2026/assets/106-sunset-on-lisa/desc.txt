Mimic - 1985

Mimic explores how shapes repeat around us, appearing in different contexts yet holding the same visual logic. The result is a minimal, graphic image that plays with form and perception. Here, the curves of a reclining body is set against a stylised graphic sunset behind the peaks, creating a quiet harmony between the body’s contours and the mountain line, so much so that, at first glance, the body is almost invisible.*
