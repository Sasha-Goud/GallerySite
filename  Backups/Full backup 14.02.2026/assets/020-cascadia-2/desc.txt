La Chiesa Madre 1999


Based on a photograph I took in Positano in 1999, this piece began as a compositional study: a telephoto lens flattens the view, turning the town’s cascading architecture into stacked geometry. Ten years later, I painted it in mostly grayscale acrylic on board, so the sunset reflections could carry the colour. The result is a balanced image with a tone that sits comfortably in almost any environment.
