Despair - 1976

This piece belongs to my 'Still in Motion' series, a body of work I was deeply engaged in during the 1970s as I explored ways of capturing movement within a still image. Though photographed in colour, it reads as almost monochrome, with only the faintest hint of tint. The effect comes from a long exposure combined with intentional camera movement, creating a blurred gesture that conveys emotion through form rather than detail.
