One - 1975 - 1998

This painting is based on a photograph taken in 1975 and completed as a mixed-media piece in 1998. It’s part of a series that explores the human body through simple, graphic compositions, often using just two tones to focus on shape rather than detail. Negative space plays an equal role, with empty areas suggesting as much as the visible forms. What’s left out is completed by the viewer, yet the image still conveys emotion and presence.
