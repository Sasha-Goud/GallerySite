Flow 99 - 1991

Part of a series of accidental aesthetics. The work relies on physical reactions creating forms, often repeating elsewhere in what surrounds us. Whether it’s melted cheese, the surface of a planet, or the structure of a cell, these visual patterns connect the smallest moments to the largest scales of the universe.
