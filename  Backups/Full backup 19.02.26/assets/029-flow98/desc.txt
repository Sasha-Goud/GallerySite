Flow 98 - 1991

One of two acrylic paintings exploring accidental aesthetics, this piece reflects how physical reactions create repeating patterns around us. From leaves pressed into pavement to planetary surfaces and cellular structures, these visual echoes connect the smallest moments to the vast scale of the universe.
