
Positano - 1999-2012

Originally a 35mm digital photograph taken in Positano, this work was posterised thirteen years later to sharpen lines and deepen bold blocks of colour. Inspired by Italy’s rich use of colour in its environment and architecture, it balances realism with abstraction while preserving the spirit of the original scene.
