♂ - 2025


One of two paintings, this one represents female, and the other, male. Despite the titles, the focus is on colour, rhythm, and movement. Unlike much of my work, which evolves slowly over time, these were both conceived and completed within a week, uncharacteristic for me, but refreshing in its spontaneity.
