SE1 - 2004-2023

From a photograph I took in 2004, this piece was completed as a posterised image in 2023. The original scene, shot along London’s South Bank, already had a striking composition of lines, reflections, and colour blocks. Posterising it enhanced the graphic quality I saw in the moment.
