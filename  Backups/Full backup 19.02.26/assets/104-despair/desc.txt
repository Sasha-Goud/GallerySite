Despair - 1976

Part of my Still in Motion series from the 1970s, this work explores movement within a still image through long exposure and intentional camera movement. Though photographed in colour, it reads as almost monochrome, with only the faintest hint of tint. The resulting blurred gesture conveys emotion through form rather than detail.
