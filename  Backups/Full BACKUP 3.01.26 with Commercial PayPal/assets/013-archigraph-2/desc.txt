Title: Le Marais (1978–2023)

This artwork is based on a photograph taken in 1978 in Le Marais, a historic district in Paris known for its narrow residential streets and timeless atmosphere. The image was later used as reference for an acrylic painting, completed in 2023.

The piece captures the quiet character of the area through simplified shapes and bold contrast, showing three figures walking toward the light at the end of a stone passage.
