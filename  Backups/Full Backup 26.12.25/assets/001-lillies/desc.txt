Title: Lily (1998/2019)

This artwork began as a photograph taken in 1998 and was digitally transformed into wall art in 2019. It shows a cluster of lily pads floating on still water, with soft shadows and reflections giving a calm, natural feel.

The piece is printed on canvas and available in three sizes. It can be ordered framed or unframed. Prices include delivery to the UK, Europe, and the United States.
