South Bank 1998–2012

I’ve always been fascinated by bicycles. My Bicycles series explores this theme. I find them graphically appealing even more than cars, and their exposed structure shows a kind of engineering elegance that’s both functional and beautiful.

This image was originally photographed in 1998 on the South Bank of the River Thames, looking across to the city lights. In 2012, I revisited the image and completed it through digital manipulation, transforming it into the stylised piece seen here.
