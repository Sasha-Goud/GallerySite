
Positano - 1999 – 2012

This piece began as a 35mm digital photograph taken in Positano. Thirteen years later, I transformed it into a posterised image to sharpen the lines and deepen the bold blocks of colour. Italy has always inspired me for the way colour lives in the environment, sun, stone, paint, plants, and sea, and for the organic confidence with which people colour their homes, creating a tapestry that has shaped Italian design. The result is a graphic composition that balances realism with abstraction while keeping the spirit of the original scene.
