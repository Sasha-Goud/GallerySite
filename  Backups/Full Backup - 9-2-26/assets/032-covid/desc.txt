Amsterdam - 2000 - 2020

This is based on a photograph taken in Amsterdam in 2000. Multiple shots were taken to capture people as they appeared in different windows; each representing a moment in time. Reflections in the glass, including the artist’s own, add another layer of time and space to the work. The final painting was completed in 2020.
