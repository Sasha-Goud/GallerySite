Bipolarity - 2000

This piece began in early 2000 and was completed in December, deliberately spaced within the year to mark the new millennium. It was made in short, scattered sessions over about ten months, woven into the interruptions of everyday life.

The painting reflects the world’s bipolar nature: north-south, east-west, rich-poor, black-white. Both sides are equally essential, different, yet balanced. No hierarchy. 

At its heart though, the aim is simple: bold, colourful graphics that bring life to a wall.
