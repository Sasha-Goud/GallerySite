Sole - 2007–2018

Part of my Unique Moments series, this photograph was taken late in the afternoon in 2007 in a nearly empty Parisian restaurant. Sunlight entered at just the right angle, casting a strong diagonal across the room. One rose stood in its path, while the others remained in shadow, forming a graphic image that feels almost staged. The photograph was posterised in 2018 to heighten the contrasts even further.
