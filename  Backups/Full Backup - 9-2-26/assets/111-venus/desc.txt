Venus - 1978

This piece is part of my Still in Motion series, where I use long exposure and deliberate camera movement, what I think of as “painting with the camera.” The original photograph was taken in 1978 as an analogue study of motion and body form.

Years later, with digital editing, I returned to the image not to enhance it, but to reduce it. I stripped away colour and detail, leaving only what’s needed for recognition. What remains is a light, near-abstract impression that still conveys movement and presence, while inviting the viewer to fill in the rest.
